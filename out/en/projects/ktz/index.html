KAZAKHSTAN TEMIR ZHOLY

A clear view of every movement

Aktobe · Control centre

48 displays. One system.

Project technologies

SamsungMediaDisplayCiscoDellExtronEffekta

The challenge

Equip a rail traffic control centre with a display system for network monitoring and round-the-clock dispatch operations.

The solution

A 16 × 3 video wall of 48 Samsung displays, measuring 19.3 m wide and 2 m high. Operator workstations include a computer and dual monitors. Storex supplied, configured and commissioned the equipment.

The outcome

A shared operational picture for traffic control, with a video wall designed for continuous operation.
